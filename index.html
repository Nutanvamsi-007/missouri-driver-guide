Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC) Limits

Missouri Guide Chapter 10

Missouri strictly enforces tiered statutory BAC limits with automatic administrative license actions:

Under 21 (Zero Tolerance) .02% BAC

Any measurable alcohol triggers immediate suspension or revocation under Missouri's Zero Tolerance law.

Commercial Driver (CDL / Class E) .04% BAC

Operating a commercial vehicle at .04% or higher leads to 1-year CDL disqualification.

Adult Drivers (21 and older) .08% BAC

Legal limit for intoxication. 1st conviction: 30-day suspension + 60-day restricted permit + criminal record.

Implied Consent Law:

Refusal to submit to a chemical test of breath/blood/saliva results in an immediate 1-year license revocation and mandatory ignition interlock device (IID).

Siddens Bening Hands-Free Law (SB 398)

Missouri Guide Chapter 16

Missouri's statewide hands-free law prohibits drivers from physically holding electronic communication devices.

🚫 Strictly Prohibited
  • Holding a phone in hand or supporting with body
  • Typing, sending, or reading text messages
  • Watching or recording video
  • Reaching for a phone that requires leaving seat
✓ Permitted Uses
  • Hands-free voice-to-text / Siri / Google
  • Mounted navigation / GPS routing
  • Bluetooth single-tap to answer
  • Emergency 911 calls
